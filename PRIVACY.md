# Privacy Policy — Gallery

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Gallery is an app that shows the photos and videos stored on your device.

**What the app accesses.** With your permission, Gallery reads photos and videos on your device (and, if you allow it, the location saved inside photo metadata) only to display them to you.

**What the app collects.** Nothing. Gallery has no internet permission. No photos, videos, metadata, or personal information leave your device. There are no ads, analytics, or third-party trackers.

**Favourites and settings** (favourite items, grid size) are stored locally on your device and removed when you uninstall the app.

**Sharing and editing.** When you tap Share or Edit, the item is handed to the app you choose; that app's own privacy policy applies.

**Deleting.** Delete actions go through the Android system (Trash on Android 11+), always with your confirmation.

**Children.** The app does not knowingly collect any data from anyone.
