# Frame Gallery - Child Safety Standards

Frame Gallery is an offline gallery app that displays photos and videos stored on the user's own device.

- The app has no social features, no user accounts, no chat, no sharing or uploading to other users, and no public content.
- Photos and videos stay on the device and are never uploaded, published or shared by the app.
- Frame Gallery has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM).
- We comply with all applicable child safety laws.
- If we become aware of any CSAE or CSAM related to the app, we will take action and report it to the relevant national and regional authorities.
